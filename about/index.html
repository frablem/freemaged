About Freemaged

A small, lightweight HTML tool for quick image edits. Made by Fran.

How it works

Resize, crop, draw, adjust, and export images right in your browser. No account or installation needed.

All image processing happens on your device. Files stay in your browser's memory while you work, and downloads are created there too. Images are never uploaded to a server.

What gets saved

Your browser remembers your theme, last-used tab and export preset, plus saved and favorite presets. These settings stay on your device.

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies. It does not receive your images or the edits you make.

Open source

The editor is a single HTML file with no external libraries. Download it to edit images offline, or look through the code on GitHub.
